daily = df.groupby("date").agg(rides=("ride_id", "count"), temp=("temperature_c", "mean")).dropna()
corr = daily[["rides", "temp"]].corr().iloc[0, 1]

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(8, 4.5))
ax.scatter(daily.temp, daily.rides, s=14, color=GREY, alpha=0.7)
b = pd.cut(daily.temp, bins=[-10, 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30])
binmean = daily.groupby(b, observed=True).rides.mean()

Summary¶

  1. Commuter city: 08:00 (3,310 rides) and 17:00 (3,826) are the twin peaks; Wednesday is the busiest day (5,834), Sunday the quietest (4,724).
  2. Short trips dominate: median 13.1 min, 58.6% of rides under 15 min.
  3. Central Square is the hub: 5,153 starts (13.5% of all rides); Castle Hill the quietest at 1,591.
  4. Weather drives demand: daily rides vs mean temperature r = 0.82; sub-zero days average 25 rides/day vs 225 on days above 20 C.
  5. Casuals pay for everything: 39.7% of rides, 98.5% of revenue ($66,228 of $67,244); members ride 12.3 min avg vs casuals 21.4 min.
  6. Anomalies: 14-Jun-2025 event spike (1,050 rides, 16x median, 90% casual); 9.5% same-station round trips; a July Fahrenheit logging fault (fixed); 112 member rides with surcharges.
