Key findings¶

  1. Strong weekday commute pattern. Wednesday is the busiest day (5,869 rides) and 17:00 the busiest hour (3,853 rides — 10.0% of all rides); 02:00 is dead (151 rides, 4% of the peak).
  2. Temperature drives demand. Daily volume correlates with temperature at r = 0.82; warm days (>20 °C) average 227 rides/day vs 33 on cold days (<10 °C) — a 6.8× difference.
  3. Central Square dominates. 10,580 rides (5,191 out / 5,389 in), 27.5% of all rides, vs Castle Hill at the bottom (3,169).
  4. Members ride more but pay almost nothing. Members are 59.8% of rides but only 1.5% of the $67,746 total fare; casuals (39.3%) generate 97.5% of revenue, and members peak at 08:00 (commute) while casuals peak at 12:00.
  5. 95 "parked bike" rides. 0.25% of rides ran 28.6–119.9 h (up to 5 days) with zero rides between 3.3 h and 28.6 h — bikes left docked, e.g. R134629: 0.92 km over 119.9 h.
