7. Anomalies¶

a) Fahrenheit readings concentrated in July¶

clean.py converted 6,495 temperatures > 50 °C as Fahrenheit. Every one of the 6,628 such raw readings falls in July 2025 — a logging-config changeover for exactly one month. Below: raw temperature medians around July (pre-clean).

In [8]:
import re
raw = pd.read_csv('rides.csv', usecols=['started_at','temperature_c'], dtype=str)
raw['t'] = pd.to_numeric(raw.temperature_c, errors='coerce')
def _m(s):
    if re.match(r'^\d{10}$', s): return pd.to_datetime(int(s), unit='s').month
    if re.match(r'^\d{2}/', s):  return pd.to_datetime(s, format='%d/%m/%Y %H:%M').month
    return pd.to_datetime(s).month
raw['m'] = raw.started_at.map(_m)
jul = raw[raw.t > 50]
print(f"raw readings > 50C: {(raw.t>50).sum():,}; in July: {(jul.m==7).sum():,} "
      f"({(jul.m==7).mean():.1%}) — i.e. {((jul.m==7).sum()/ (raw.m==7).sum()):.0%} of all July readings")
print(f"July raw median was {raw[raw.m==7].t.median():.1f} vs June {raw[raw.m==6].t.median():.1f} "
      f"and Aug {raw[raw.m==8].t.median():.1f} (a 'spike' of +50C that is really a unit bug)")

raw readings > 50C: 6,628; in July: 6,628 (100.0%) — i.e. 98% of all July readings

b) TEST-DEPOT synthetic traffic¶

216 raw rows are 2-minute depot-to-depot hops with 0 km and $0 fare — removed. Also: 812 exact duplicate rows (2.1% of the file) and 200 reversed start/end timestamps, both repaired/dropped.
