Takeaways¶

  • Commuter peaks at 08:00 (3,310) and 17:00 (3,826 rides); weekends are only ~8% quieter.
  • Durations are right-skewed: median 13.1 min, p95 36.6 min.
  • Central Square is the hub with 10,495 boardings+alightings.
  • Temperature explains most seasonal variation (r = 0.82); June has 9.5x January's volume.
  • Members are 59.8% of rides, 12.3 min avg vs casuals' 21.4 min.
  • See FINDINGS.md for the headline numbers.
