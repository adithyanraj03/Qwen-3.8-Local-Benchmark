Bike-Share Ride Analysis — 2025¶

Analysis of rides_clean.parquet (38,427 rides, 12 stations, 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31). The raw export rides.csv was cleaned first; see clean.py and CLEANING.md for the data issues found and the steps taken.

Sections:

  1. Ride volume by hour and day of week
  2. Ride-duration distribution
  3. Busiest stations
  4. Temperature vs. ride volume
  5. Member vs. casual riders
  6. Anomalies

2. Ride-duration distribution¶

95 rides (0.25%) lasted more than 12 hours — bikes taken out and returned days later ("forgotten bikes", up to 119.9 h). They are excluded from the distribution below so the typical ride is visible; they are examined in the anomalies section.

6. Anomalies discovered¶

Beyond the cleaning issues (duplicates, test rides, swapped timestamps, Fahrenheit readings in July - see CLEANING.md), the following stand out in the clean data:

  1. Seasonality - a 9.4x spread between January (744) and June (7,010).
  2. Forgotten bikes - 95 rides longer than 12 h, up to 119.9 h (5 days).
  3. The 14 June surge - a single day with 1,057 rides, 910 of them starting at Harbor Front (~60x its usual daily volume).
  4. Extreme single rides - longest distance 48.33 km, highest fare $30.78.
